Building the RCMS operations platform

Four applications on one data layer, built in nine gated phases. Every phase ends in software you could ship on its own, so the build can pause after any of them without leaving a half-finished product.

Locked decisions

What you chose, and what it means

Scope against budget

Full architecture, raised budget

All four applications as specified. This is a 6 to 7 month build, not a 10 week one. The ₹50,000 figure from the earlier plan buys roughly Phase 0 and Phase 1.

Foundation

Continue the current repository

Nothing gets thrown away. The public site, hero, Governance Room, design system and telemetry model already exist and are the defensible part. We add a database, auth and payments beneath them.

Payments

Razorpay only at launch

UPI, cards, netbanking and GST invoicing for an Indian learner base. Stripe gets added in Phase 8 only if international corporate clients actually appear.

Build order

Student and Admin together, thin

Phase 2 closes the whole loop at minimum depth: an enquiry becomes a lead, a lead becomes a paying student, a student takes a lesson, and you see all of it in the admin. Depth comes after.

The real bottleneck is not code

The platform can be finished and still have nothing to sell. Five programs at six to fourteen weeks each is a large amount of video, SOP templates and quiz material that only you can produce. Content production should start in parallel at Phase 1, not after Phase 9, or the launch date is set by recording rather than by engineering.

The phases

What each one delivers and how we know it is done

PHASE 0

Foundations

3 weeks

Put a real backend under the site that already exists. Nothing user-visible changes except that people can create accounts.

Build

  • PostgreSQL (Neon or Supabase) with Prisma schema and migrations
  • Auth: email + password and Google, with verified email
  • Roles and permissions: visitor, student, trainer, admin, owner
  • Audit log written on every mutation, from day one
  • Seed the existing telemetry, catalog and ladder data into the database
  • Staging and production environments, secrets, CI on every push

Decisions to make here

  • Hosting: Vercel plus managed Postgres, or a single VPS
  • Transactional email provider (Resend assumed)
  • File storage for PDFs and SOPs (UploadThing or S3)
  • Error and uptime monitoring (Sentry plus a status ping)

Gate: a stranger can register, verify their email, sign in and land on an empty portal; every write is in the audit log; a rollback of the last migration is tested and works.

PHASE 1

Commerce spine

2 weeks

Take money correctly, including tax and refunds, before building anything that depends on it.

Build

  • Razorpay orders, checkout, webhook verification, idempotency
  • Products mapped to the five programs, three tracks and four mentoring tiers
  • GST-compliant invoice generation and email delivery
  • Enrolment granted automatically on verified payment
  • Refund flow matching the published policy: seven days, under 20 percent accessed
  • Reconciliation view: every payment tied to a person and a product

Risks

  • Webhook replay and double enrolment — solved with idempotency keys
  • GST registration and HSN/SAC codes must be confirmed before invoices go out
  • Refund window needs the access-percentage metric, which Phase 2 provides; ship manual approval first

Gate: a real ₹2,999 payment completes end to end on production, produces a valid GST invoice, grants access, and can be refunded. Revenue is possible from this point.

PHASE 2

Thin loop: Student and Admin

4 weeks

Close the entire business loop at minimum depth, so every later phase deepens something that already works rather than adding something new.

Student Portal

  • My Academy: enrolled programs, resume where you left off
  • Lesson player: video, PDF downloads, mark complete
  • Progress framed as the ladder, not a percentage: current role, target role, skills completed
  • Profile, invoices, account settings

Admin OS, first slice

  • Leads list fed by the contact form, with owner and status
  • Students list with enrolments and payment history
  • Course and lesson records, managed but not yet authored in-app
  • Executive Overview KPI strip driven by real counts

Gate: an enquiry submitted on the public site appears as a lead, that person buys a program, watches a lesson, and all of it is visible in the admin without touching the database.

PHASE 3

Course engine and Trainer Studio

4 weeks

Stop needing a developer to publish a course. This is what turns the platform into an asset you operate rather than a site you commission.

Build

  • Curriculum builder: drag modules and lessons, reorder, draft and publish states
  • Video pipeline with signed playback URLs, not public links
  • Resource library: SOPs, templates, benchmark sheets, per-lesson attachments
  • Quiz builder: multiple choice and scenario questions, pass thresholds
  • Learning outcomes, difficulty, estimated duration per module

Decisions to make here

  • Video host: Bunny Stream, Mux or Cloudflare Stream — cost and DRM differ sharply
  • Whether quizzes gate progression or only inform
  • Watermarking policy for downloadable SOPs

Gate: you create and publish a complete module — video, resources, quiz — without a developer, and a student sees it immediately.

PHASE 4

Governance Labs

3 weeks

Turn the Governance Room from a demonstration into assessed coursework. This is the differentiator, and it is the reason the platform is not an LMS.

Build

  • Scenario engine: multiple client datasets, not one hard-coded engagement
  • Per-student lab state saved: capacity settings, action register edits, RCA answers
  • Executive Notes become a submitted, gradeable artefact
  • Trainer review surface with rubric and written feedback
  • Lab results attached to the student's ladder progression

Why this matters

  • Nothing in any competing platform does this
  • It converts 21 years of judgement into a repeatable assessment
  • It is the strongest argument for corporate pricing in Phase 8

Gate: a student completes a full governance review against a scenario they have not seen, submits executive notes, and a trainer grades it against a rubric.

PHASE 5

CRM and lead engine

4 weeks

Every enquiry becomes a scored lead with an owner and a next action. This is where the business stops leaking.

Build

  • Pipeline: new, qualified, consultation booked, won, lost
  • Lead record: role, experience, interested program, source, score, owner, notes
  • Activity timeline: page views, brochure downloads, pricing visits, replies
  • Scoring rules as specified: brochure +10, pricing ×3 +8, mentoring booked +30, WhatsApp reply +20, purchase +100
  • WhatsApp: click-to-chat first, Business API once volume justifies the approval process
  • Email sequences on Resend, unsubscribe handled properly

Risks

  • WhatsApp Business API approval takes weeks and needs a verified business — start the application at Phase 3
  • Behavioural scoring needs analytics events from Phase 2; verify they are captured before building rules on them
  • Consent and data retention: leads are personal data under DPDP

Gate: no enquiry from any channel can exist without an owner and a scored record, and you can answer "what happened to that person" for any lead in under ten seconds.

PHASE 6

Finance and analytics

3 weeks

Make the Executive Overview real. Every number on it traces to a row you can click into.

Build

  • Revenue by source: programs, tracks, mentoring, corporate
  • MRR and ARR where subscriptions exist, cash revenue where they do not
  • Refunds, failed payments, outstanding invoices
  • Funnel: visitor, lead, qualified, paid, with conversion at each step
  • Course performance: enrolments, completion, drop-off point per lesson
  • Trainer utilisation and mentoring revenue per trainer

Principle

  • Same standard as the Governance Room: every metric states what changed, why, how much it matters, who owns it and what happens next
  • No vanity metrics on the overview

Gate: you can close a month from the admin alone — revenue by source, refunds, conversion, top programs — without exporting anything to a spreadsheet.

PHASE 7

Mentoring operations

3 weeks

Replace the booking placeholder with a real scheduling system, and make sessions produce artefacts rather than just happening.

Build

  • Trainer availability rules, timezone-correct, IST and US shift friendly
  • Slot booking with payment, confirmation email and WhatsApp message
  • Reschedule and cancel honouring the 24 hour policy automatically
  • Session record: pre-read artefact, notes, action plan, follow-up date
  • Calendar views: day, week, month, per trainer

Decisions to make here

  • Google Calendar sync, or self-contained
  • Video call: external link, or embedded
  • Whether trainers other than you exist at launch

Gate: a mentoring session is booked, paid, held, documented and followed up entirely inside the platform, with no Calendly and no manual WhatsApp coordination.

PHASE 8

Community, corporate and hardening

3 weeks

Open the community, enable corporate cohorts, and make the whole thing safe to leave running.

Build

  • Five moderated channels, threads, reporting, moderator tools
  • Organisation accounts: seats, manager dashboard, cohort progress
  • Corporate invoicing and purchase orders
  • Stripe added only if international corporate demand is real

Hardening

  • Security review: authorisation on every route, rate limiting, secrets rotation
  • Accessibility audit to WCAG AA across all four applications
  • Performance: Core Web Vitals on public pages, query budgets on admin
  • Backups with a tested restore, not just a backup
  • Load test at ten times expected concurrency

Gate: restore from backup into a clean environment succeeds, the security review has no open high findings, and the accessibility audit passes AA.

What most likely goes wrong

RiskWhy it bitesMitigation

WhatsApp Business API approvalTakes weeks, needs a verified business, and Phase 5 assumes itApply during Phase 3. Ship click-to-chat as the fallback and keep it permanently

Blocking

What I need from you before Phase 0 starts

  1. Hosting and database choice, or permission to chooseVercel plus Neon is the fastest path and costs roughly ₹2,000 a month at launch scale. A VPS is cheaper and more work. I will pick Vercel plus Neon unless you say otherwise.
  2. Domain and business entityThe registered domain, the legal entity name, GST registration number and the HSN/SAC code for training services. Invoices cannot be generated without these.
  3. Razorpay accountMerchant account with KYC completed, and test-mode keys shared through a password manager, never in chat.
  4. Real founder credentialsStill outstanding from earlier: years, engagements led, team sizes, collections under governance. I removed the invented figures and will not put numbers on the site that you have not confirmed.
  5. WhatsApp number for the businessCurrently a placeholder in the code. Needed for click-to-chat now and for the API application in Phase 3.
  6. Which program is content-ready firstPhase 2 needs one real program with real lessons to be a genuine test rather than a demo. Foundation is the obvious candidate.
  7. Who else will build thisThe estimates assume one full-time developer. If it stays only me in these sessions, the calendar stretches and the plan should be resequenced to put revenue-earning phases even earlier.
