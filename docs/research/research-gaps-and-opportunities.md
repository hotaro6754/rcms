# Research gaps and opportunities

Opportunities are the owner's gaps 1–6 from the brief, each mapped to evidence and current status.
These are product hypotheses, not claims that competitors lack them.

| Gap | Evidence | Priority | Status in the product |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. From content to demonstrated competence | P1–P3 (`practitioner-pain-points.md`) | Critical | **Built for denials:** case simulator with rubric, critical errors, outcomes |
| 2. Visual workflows that explain decisions | P2, P3 | Critical | **Partial:** SVG workflow strip, claim anatomy and timeline in the workbench; no general diagram engine or branching (multi-step consequence) simulator |
| 3. Evidence-based readiness | P2, P4 | Critical | **Built:** competency framework; outcomes only from scored cases; manager matrix |
| 4. Help and escalation in the workflow | P2 | High | **Built:** assistant with sources and hand-off; tickets from lessons and results; routing and SLA |
| 5. Skills linked to operational outcomes | P4 | High | **Not built:** work-queue, aging and underpayment simulations |
| 6. Trustworthy, maintainable content | S20; governance policy | High | **Built:** review before publish (courses, library, cases, competencies), versions, sources, review dates |
