## Comparison (what each is for, not a feature checklist)

| | What it offers (per source) | What to learn | Where RCMS differs (hypothesis) |
|---|---|---|---|
| HFMA CRCR | Revenue-cycle fundamentals credential; $399 non-member listed (U6; S5 corroborates) | Recognised body, defined assessment, maintenance | Practice-heavy cases that complement, not replace, a credential |
| AAPC CPB | Billing training incl. payer rules, A/R follow-up, redacted-record practice (U8; S1) | Structured path tied to a credential | Scored decisions on synthetic denials; team view of competency |
| Waystar | Clearance, claims, denial recovery, analytics (U9) | Workflows are connected end to end | Teach the reasoning behind the categories; do not rebuild production software |
| Experian Health | Denial workflow, prioritisation, root-cause templates (U10, U11) | A denial is a case: evidence, category, action, outcome | Learners operate a safe simulation of that reasoning |
| Optum | RCM operations, A/R recovery, denial management (U12) | Audience includes analysts and leaders | Progression from task to audit and team performance |
| LMS platforms (ClassroomIO, Thinkific, TalentLMS, HealthStream) | Course building, certificates, org management (`github-landscape.md`; S11, S25–S30) | These are table stakes | RCM-specific cases, competency evidence, support built in |

## Conclusion
No claim is made that competitors lack simulations; public pages do not show every capability.
The testable position is: *a role-based RCM operations academy where people learn through realistic
synthetic cases, receive evidence-based feedback, and demonstrate readiness against a transparent
competency framework.*
