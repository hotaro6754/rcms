# User pain points

Research date: 15 October 2026. **Limitation first:** Reddit was not reachable from this
environment and X returned nothing relevant (`RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`). There is no first-hand
community evidence here. Themes below come from industry surveys, review-site summaries, job
postings and training-provider pages, each labelled with its strength:

- **Recurring**: appears in two or more independent sources.
- **Single source**: one source only; a lead, not a finding.
- **Hypothesis**: inferred from the evidence; needs interviews.

## 1. Learners entering RCM

| Theme | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Entry jobs want experience or certification that freshers lack | AR analyst posting asks for at least 6 months' experience [S19]; a US listing asks for two years of billing or claims experience (search summary); providers say certification is not legally required but preferred | Recurring |
| Short, cheap or "guaranteed job" courses are distrusted | Guide advises avoiding programs promising employment or certification in under six months, and non-affiliated programs [S20]; Indian institutes advertise "100% placement" self-reported [S14] | Recurring |
| Typical entry route in India is trainee AR calling on night shifts with on-the-job training | MedTerm RCM posting: trainee AR roles, night shift, in-person interviews [S18] | Single source |
| Learners cannot tell which credential matters | AAPC, HFMA, AAHAM and AHIMA all offer adjacent credentials at different levels [S1, S5, S8, S10] | Hypothesis |

## 2. Employers (provider revenue cycle teams and BPOs)

| Theme | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| New hires make costly claim errors | 92% of executives said so (Experian 2023 via Becker's) [S23] | Single source (vendor survey) |
| Understaffing and turnover | 43% understaffed (2025) [S21]; turnover 11–40% at 80% of organisations (2023) [S23]; MedCity on shortages [S24] | Recurring |
| Denials are rising and mostly reworked by people | 41% of providers at ≥10% denial rate; 90% of denials reworked with human review [S21] | Single source (vendor survey, two years corroborate the trend per the summary) |
| Training is seen as a denial lever | "Just over 40%" cite staff training and accountability [S22] | Single source |
| Team-scale RCM training is sold as large libraries | HealthStream: "over 2,000" RCM courses, onboarding and upskilling pathways [S11] | Single source |

## 3. Administrators of learning platforms

| Theme | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Reporting is weaker than admins want | Moodle reporting enhancements wanted [S28]; TalentLMS "limited reporting graphics" [S30]; Thinkific custom reports limited (low-confidence aggregate) [S31] | Recurring (across products) |
| Admin experience is confusing | TalentLMS reviewer: "needs an Administrator UX overhaul" [S29]; Moodle setup and admin effort [S28] | Recurring |
| Course builders are basic | TalentLMS "course builder basic" [S30] | Single source |
| Billing and cancellation friction with course platforms | Trustpilot themes per a low-confidence aggregate [S31]; a refused refund in one Thinkific review | Single source |
