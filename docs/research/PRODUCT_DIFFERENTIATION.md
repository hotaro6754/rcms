# Product differentiation

Research date: 15 October 2026. Every differentiator below is a **proposal** grounded in the
evidence in `COMPETITIVE_LANDSCAPE.md` and `USER_PAIN_POINTS.md`. "Not seen elsewhere" means not
seen **in the sources I could reach**; it is not a claim of uniqueness.

## Positioning

> Job-ready RCM practice and operations governance, assessed on realistic synthetic scenarios,
> complementary to AAPC / HFMA / AAHAM credentials rather than a replacement for them.

What we must never say: accredited, certified by a professional body, HIPAA-certified, guaranteed
placement.

## Differentiators

### D1. Assessed scenario practice, not attendance
- **Problem:** employers report new-hire claim errors [S23]; learners distrust attendance-based courses [S20].
- **Evidence:** credentials test knowledge in MCQ exams [S6, S8]; course marketplaces sell video.
- **Alternatives:** AAPC practice tests; institute classroom drills.
- **Why ours may be better:** scenario stems, matching, sequencing and written case analysis graded against a released version, with remediation links back to lessons (built).
- **Cost:** low incremental (engine built); content authoring is the real cost.
- **Measure:** first-attempt pass rate per competency; employer-reported time to productivity (interview).

### D2. Verifiable certificates with an evidence trail
- **Problem:** certificate credibility [S20].
- **Evidence:** Open Badges 3.0 / VC 2.0 is the emerging standard for self-verifying credentials [S32].
- **Why ours may be better:** today: public serial page showing course version and status, revocation that still resolves (built). Next: Open Badges 3.0 export.
- **Cost:** medium for signed badges.
- **Measure:** verification page views per certificate; employer verification requests.

### D3. A leadership and governance level
- **Problem:** turnover and understaffing push team leads into management without training [S21, S23] (hypothesis that this is a training gap).
- **Evidence:** only AAHAM's CRCE was found at executive level [S9]; no governance content seen in HealthStream or institute results.
- **Why ours may be better:** Leadership level (SLA/KPI, capacity, governance) plus the existing Governance Labs simulations.
- **Cost:** content-heavy; platform built.
- **Measure:** Leadership enrolments from corporate accounts; lab completion.

### D4. Team plans with manager visibility for BPOs
- **Problem:** BPO onboarding at scale with high turnover [S18, S23].
- **Evidence:** HealthStream sells team libraries in the US [S11]; no team features seen for Indian institutes.
- **Why ours may be better:** organisations, seats, group assignment and manager progress view (built), priced in INR.
- **Cost:** low for what exists; self-service seat purchase not built.
- **Measure:** seats per organisation; 30/60/90-day completion by group.

### D5. Reviewed, dated content
- **Problem:** payer rules and regulations change; stale content misleads.
- **Evidence:** credential bodies require recertification [S7], signalling that currency matters.
- **Why ours may be better:** academic and compliance review by different people, effective and review dates, release history (built); public governance policy page (this round).
- **Cost:** low.
- **Measure:** share of published lessons past their review date (compliance dashboard).
