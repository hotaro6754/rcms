# Market landscape

Updated 16 October 2026. Summary of `COMPETITIVE_LANDSCAPE.md`, `competitor-analysis.md` and
`PRODUCT_DIFFERENTIATION.md`.

**Segments (all hypotheses, none validated):** trainees entering RCM; experienced staff moving into
denial, AR or analyst roles; team leads and managers; RCM BPOs; provider L&D teams; independent
instructors. Price signals observed: Indian institutes ₹10,999–₹44,500 [S15, S16]; AAPC CPB from
$1,495 [S1]; AAPC CE courses $149–$299 [S3, S4]; HFMA CRCR $399 [S5, U6].

**Not established:** total addressable market, willingness to pay, conversion rates, employer
recognition. No figures for these appear in this repository, by design.

**Where the market is crowded:** course libraries, certificates, LMS features.
**Where evidence points to a gap:** demonstrated job competence on realistic cases, manager-visible
readiness, help and escalation inside the learning workflow (`research-gaps-and-opportunities.md`).
