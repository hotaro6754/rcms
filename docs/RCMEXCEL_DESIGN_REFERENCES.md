## Owner's Cosmos board — cosmos.so/harshith6754/rcmexcel-visual-research (inspected)

What it is mostly made of: information design. Sankey flows, an exploded stack of coloured layers, concentric rings with callout leaders, numbered step flows (01–04 pills), a red-circle path infographic, a Gantt-style timeline, report and dashboard pages, two monoline icon sets, a "FINE LINE" route-and-circle collage, folder tabs in warm sand and coral, and a few editorial flat illustrations.

- Learn: diagrams carry the story; muted, warm-neutral palettes; numbered steps; callouts with leader lines; layered "exploded" views to show structure.
- Do not copy: any specific poster, dashboard or icon set. Use the grammar (rings, flows, layers, steps), not the artwork.
- Fit with the site: the ten-stage ring and the navy/sage/sky/sand palette already speak this language. The generated people scenes do not (F015).
