The operating manual for revenue cycle careers

RCMExcel should look like a well-made annual report crossed with a reference manual: big confident numbers, real documents and diagrams, one deep green brand field, and navy type on paper. Built from the owner's Cosmos board plus outside research.

§ 01Board readout

What the board says about your taste

The board is not minimal. It is rich in information: charts, stat tiles, report spreads, process maps. It leans on two colour families, deep green and navy, warmed with cream and a coral highlight. Clean brand systems on print (folders, binders, stationery) show up again and again. Most of it fits the brief. About one save in ten pulls the other way (gradients, crypto, generic flat-vector people) and I have set those aside. The ink-line faces and characters stay, for students only.

§ 02Thesis

One sentence to judge every screen by

If a page could not be printed and handed to a CFO or a hospital billing director without embarrassment, it is not done.

That rules out glows, glass and floating card piles on its own. It rules in what your father already trusts: remittance advices, aging reports, month-end operating reports. Every screen borrows the conventions of those documents: section codes, figure numbers, effective dates, ledger tables, footnotes.

§ 03Colour

Navy for words and data, green for the brand, coral only when something needs a look

Ink navy#0E1F3D · from the logo bars. All text, all chart marks.
Clinical green#0F4C3A · brand fields, current lesson, primary action. Bridges the logo teal and the board's greens.
Mint#D6EADF · completed states, quiet panels.
Paper#F3F3EF · page ground. Neutral, not yellow cream.
Coral#E2603A · the one highlighted bar, an overdue item, a denial. Never decoration.

Proportion on a typical screen: paper 62 · sheets 20 · navy 12 · green 5 · coral 1

Dark mode flips to a navy night ground with mint-green and soft coral, so the admin overview can still be read at 11 pm. No gradients in either mode.

§ 09Illustration

Three kinds of drawing, each with its own place

The board has three illustration styles worth keeping. The four faces with loose ink lines, the ink-line character on the magazine contents page, and the billboard figures share one trick: a flat colour shape with a single loose black line for the face and limbs. The health-ecosystem page shows the other good style: people drawn in ink line with muted fills, one per role, in labelled columns. Generic flat-vector people (the clinic scene, the nurse and doctor on purple) are the look to avoid.

Tier 1 · Diagrams

Every lesson and report

One line weight, numbered steps, mono callouts, coral on the failure point. No people. This is most of the drawing on the site.

Tier 2 · Role figures

Course pages and the public site

Ink-line people with flat navy, green and mint fills, one per RCM role: front desk, coder, biller, AR specialist, manager. Shown in labelled columns, like the board's health-ecosystem page.

Tier 3 · Mascot

Student moments only

"The Bars": four characters made from the logo's rising bars, with ink-line faces. They appear for welcome, quiz feedback, empty states, streaks and certificates. Never on admin screens, never inside lesson content.

§ 11Grid and surface

Grid, rules and corners

  • GRID12 columns, 1320px max. A 2-column margin holds section codes and figure labels, as on the board's Swiss report grids.
  • SPACE4px base: 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 72. Sections separate with space and a rule, not boxes.
  • RULES1px hairlines between rows. One 2px navy rule under each page title, like a report masthead.
  • CORNERS0 to 4px. Paper documents have square corners. Buttons get 4px.
  • SHADOWNone, except menus and the video player, which float.
  • FIELDSOne solid colour field per screen at most: the green "continue" sheet for students, nothing for admins.
§ 12Page by page

What changes on each screen

  • Coming soonPaper ground, logo, one Newsreader line, the four levels as rising bars drawn in navy with the current one in green. Remove the gradient text, glows and drifting tiles.
  • Sign inSplit page: left a green field with one statement and the level bars, right a plain white form. No gradient.
  • Student deskGreen "continue" sheet, week streak, course contents in manual style with dotted leaders and lesson lengths, four-level path as rising bars.
  • Lesson playerKeep the collapsible rail and Video / Slides / Reading tabs. Rail becomes the table of contents (FIG. 05.2). Lesson title in Newsreader, codes in mono.
  • Admin overviewAnnual-report spread: KPI strip with big light numbers (FIG. 05.1), one highlighted bar, exceptions list with Act / Watch / OK, course ledger table, activity log.
  • ReportsEach report reads as a printed page: title, period, figures with one-line explanations, a ledger table, a footnote on how numbers are counted.
  • Admins and studentsTwo ledger tables, search on top. No avatars-and-cards grid.
  • Course editorFormat status (Video / Slides / Reading) as a three-cell row per lesson, filled in navy when present, coral outline when missing.
§ 13Motion

Motion only where it explains something

  • 01Bars grow once on first load of a chart, 400ms, then stay still.
  • 02Process diagrams step through when a lesson figure scrolls into view, one node at a time, so the order is the lesson.
  • 03Rail and tabs slide 180ms. Everything else is instant. Reduced-motion users get no animation.

§ 15Avoid

Never on RCMExcel

  • ✕Gradients, glows, glass panels, purple, neon, 3D renders and blobs.
  • ✕Stock doctors with stethoscopes, smiling-patient heroes, and generic flat-vector people with tiny heads and long limbs.
  • ✕Mascots on admin screens or inside lesson content. They belong to student moments only.
  • ✕Rows of identical rounded cards with icons in coloured circles.
  • ✕Coral or green used as decoration. Each colour has one job.
  • ✕Retail promo tone (banners, 50% OFF, carousels) as seen on competitor sites.
§ 16Sign-off
Three choices only you can make
  1. Green as the second brand colour? The board is full of deep green; the logo is navy and teal. This direction uses green as the bridge.
  2. Mascot "The Bars" for students? Built from the logo bars, drawn in loose ink line (§ 09). Yes, no, or a different character.
  3. Serif headlines? Newsreader for titles gives the editorial, report feel. The alternative is all-grotesk, which reads more like software.
