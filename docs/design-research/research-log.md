### Q: "swiss graphic design"
Results (OBS): Swiss Style poster grids (black and white, red construction lines), orange
geometric Swiss poster, Zürich Card transit ad, Swiss flag red grid, editorial spreads.

- C01 · Element https://www.cosmos.so/e/1983391342 · Source: Stephen Kelman, "Swiss Style A0
  Poster Grid System for InDesign" https://stephenkelman.co.uk/swiss-style-a0-poster-grid-system-for-indesign
  · Saved by 63; in collections "Typography" (@stephenkelman), "Swiss Design" (@noahwainwright),
  "Layout inspiration" (@nicotinemars).
  OBS: black A0 sheet, 4-column grid drawn as thin red construction lines, oversized neo-grotesque
  "Swiss Style" set flush-left, small 4-column text block across the top with spec metadata
  (size, ISO, baseline grid, margins).
  INT: shows how visible grid + metadata can become the decoration itself. For RCMExcel: course and
  KPI pages can expose their structure (module numbers, codes, effective dates) as typographic
  metadata instead of using illustrations.

- C02 · https://www.cosmos.so/e/1457710852 · One Medical identity (repost via identity.nae-design.com).
  OBS: deep green stationery, lowercase sans wordmark, small four-dot cross mark, nothing else.
  INT: a healthcare brand can be calm and confident with one colour field and a tiny mark.

## Education platforms
- E01 · Harvard Business School Online · https://online.hbs.edu/
  OBS: dark header with crimson; hero "Learn Online from the Leaders in Business Education" in bold sans
  beside an instructor video; course categories as three/four-up cards, each with an editorial collage
  illustration (halftone photo cut-outs + black geometric shapes + small circle motifs) on one flat pastel
  per subject (yellow, orange, pink, mint, blue, green, lilac); bottom sticky offer bar.
  INT: a consistent illustration system per subject is how an academy gets identity without stock photos.
  RCMExcel could give each level (Beginner/Professional/Leader/Executive) one flat tone + documentary
  cut-outs of RCM artefacts (claims, EOBs, KPI sheets). Avoid the bright pastel range.
  Scores: distinction 8, type 7, composition 8, brand 9, clarity 8, relevance 8.

  icons (training courses, membership, books, code look-up app), category tab strip, floating HELP button.
  VERDICT: retail/promotional register; the gap RCMExcel can win by looking institutional and calm.
